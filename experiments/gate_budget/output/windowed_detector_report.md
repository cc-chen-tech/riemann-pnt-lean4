## Interpretation

- If mean S scales like T^-(1-h) polylog, the windowed detector
  threshold is beaten by the aligned seed response
  x^gap * log X / T0 for every gap > 0: the window H dilutes the
  frequency-weighted complementary mass.
- max S and the bad fraction control the detection-point choice
  lemma (there exists gamma in the window with S(gamma) small);
  the bad fraction must stay bounded away from 1.

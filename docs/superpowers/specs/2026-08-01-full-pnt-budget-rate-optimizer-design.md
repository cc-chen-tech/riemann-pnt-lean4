# Full PNT Budget Rate Optimizer

## Goal

Optimize the complete machine-checked relative PNT upper budget over the actual
finite Pintz good-height rate grid and derive relative PNT decay.

## Concrete cost

For rate `k` and natural sample `m`, use

```text
naturalPointPintzPNTHybridCeilingRelativeUpperBudget
  selection.constant
  (pintzCarlsonGoodHeightBase k m)
  (actual candidate height at k)
  k m 0
  singletonZeroThresholdBucketInput
```

This includes the hybrid density term, real-ordinate residual, fixed zeta
constant, trivial-zero term, and actual cofinal contour remainder.

## Pointwise optimizer

Select the exact minimum over the finite rate set at every natural sample.
The optimized height is the actual good-height candidate generated by the
selected rate. The truncated formula certificate and density comparisons prove
that the real relative PNT error is bounded by this minimum budget.

## Decay argument

The optimizer budget is no larger than the budget at the least positive rate.
Under `baseRate <= 1` and the existing Pintz density-gap condition, that fixed
base-rate budget tends to zero. Eventual nonnegativity follows from its real
error upper bound. Squeezing yields decay of the minimum budget and then decay
of the real relative PNT error.

## Boundary

This proves an exact finite-rate optimum, not continuous-rate optimality or a
sharp Johnston/Bellotti constant. It does not prove unconditional Omega or RH.

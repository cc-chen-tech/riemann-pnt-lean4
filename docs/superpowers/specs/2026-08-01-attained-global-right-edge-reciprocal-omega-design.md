# Attained global right-edge reciprocal Omega design

The predicate `IsAttainedGlobalNontrivialZeroRealPart rho` records both that
`rho` is a nontrivial zeta zero and that every nontrivial zero has real part at
most `rho.re`.  This is an explicit maximum-attainment hypothesis, not merely
the existence of a supremum.

The conjugate pair `{rho, conj rho}` is automatically a nonempty target-line
seed.  Global maximality supplies the outside-cluster real-part cap, so
Stack163 yields an `Omega_+` or `Omega_-` witness at scale `x^(rho.re)`.

Strict exclusion of real-ordinate zeros at the same real part remains
explicit.  The theorem does not prove that the global supremum is attained,
does not prove both signs, and does not imply RH.

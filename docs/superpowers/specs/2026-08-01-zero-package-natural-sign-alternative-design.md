# Natural zero-package sign alternative

## Result

An unsigned natural-point far witness with nonnegative amplitude cannot keep
changing which sign supplies every distant witness without one sign occurring
arbitrarily far.  Classically,

`unsigned witness -> positive witness or negative witness`.

Applying this to the naturally sampled actual equal-real-part zeta-zero
package gives a fixed one-sided oscillation alternative at the exact
`sqrt(energy)` scale.

## Claim boundary

The result proves `Omega+` or `Omega-` as a disjunction, not both signs.  A
simultaneous `Omega+-` theorem still requires additional phase recurrence or a
quantitative signed anti-cancellation theorem.  No claim about RH is made.

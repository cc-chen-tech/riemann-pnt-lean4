# Power-to-Square-Root-Log Amplitude Transfer

## Objective

Reuse the repository's power-normalized closed-term estimates to close two of
the three rate-independent residual components at every square-root-log
exponential scale.

## Theorem chain

1. Invert the established contour-to-power divergence theorem to prove that a
   fixed power amplitude divided by `exp(-s sqrt(log m))` tends to zero.
2. Transfer any natural-point power-normalized negligible remainder to the
   square-root-log amplitude by multiplying normalized ratios.
3. Apply the transfer to the closed logarithmic and actual real-axis terms.
4. Reduce full residual normalization to the single explicit
   `sqrt(log m) / m` tail ratio.

## Claim boundary

This slice does not yet prove the tail ratio. It closes only the two existing
power-negligible closed terms and records the exact remaining input.

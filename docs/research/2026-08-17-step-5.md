This document analyzes Step 5 of the chain:

iteratively refined to show ζ actually has ZERO zeros with `Re > β'`
for some `β' > β`.

## Step 5 mechanics

To compress to zero, we need INFINITE ITERATION.  Each iteration tightens

improvement at iteration n.

The improvement `ε_n` depends on:
1. The seed-deleted residual lemma's coefficient `c > 1/2`
2. The framework's `c - 1/2` (after subtracting the loss term)
3. The omega witness construction

For the iteration to converge to `β_∞ = 1`, we need `Σ ε_n = 1 - β`
(finite total improvement).  This requires `ε_n → 0` as `n → ∞`.

But the framework's constants are FIXED.  The improvement at each
iteration depends on the framework's machinery, which doesn't depend on
n.  So if the improvement is constant `ε > 0`, the iteration reaches
`β = 1` in finitely many steps.

- Step 1: Choose cluster S = {finite zeros with Re > β}.

The improvement `ε` at each step depends on:
